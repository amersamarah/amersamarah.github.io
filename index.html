What I care about

Wireline transceivers, mixed-signal loops, CDR and PLL, DRAM timing, and analog CAD. I like problems where the circuit, the model, and the lab disagree — and the job is to find which one is lying.

Lately that has meant LPDDR bring-up, PCIe link behavior, PVT sensors, and building small tools that turn a netlist or a timing report into something a person can actually read.
